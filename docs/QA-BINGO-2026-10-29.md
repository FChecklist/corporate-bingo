# Corporate Bingo: QA report, 29 Oct 2026

Written by the cloud QA session `qa/bingo-cloud-2026-10-29` (cut from `boss/docs-2026-10-29`, which is the live commit `bd66d3c` = release 1.3.2 plus the docs/boss folder). Plain English. Nothing was deployed, merged, paid or sent. No secrets are in this file or in any file of the branch.

## 1. The answer in five lines
1. The game rules and the engine are sound: a new model (`bun tools/qa-model.mjs`, 2,595 option combinations), 300 house-by-game cases against an independent oracle, and the correctness audit (all 10 prize shapes, ties, 3,000 random call schedules) found no wrong winner, wrong tie or wrong prize order.
2. 34 real defects were found and fixed (31 in the product, 3 in tests), each with a test that fails before and passes after (section 4). The worst: a Host's AI link could fetch every guest's name and number; two ways switched off the 20-hour erasure; the organizer's AI key could put a game live and use up a bought pack; the report lost the company name and counted numbers wrongly; organizers and AI helpers were told the wrong deadlines; the AI was taught Tambola prizes; the word "private" was in 27 places; "Only my employees" games gave guests no way to join; a finger on the screen could swallow a tap.
3. The free 10-person demo works end to end through the real endpoints (section 6). In a real browser 116 of 134 scenarios pass; the 18 that fail also fail on the live commit or fail only because this machine cannot reach the random-number servers (section 7).
4. 22 items need the owner and were NOT guessed (section 5). The ones that matter most: the winner "override" that the Terms say does not exist (found by two audits); the test sign-ins on the live site that give free 1,000-person games until 8 Nov; winners decided by phones that a person with three fake phones can fake; no claim step; the legal pages that say "23 hours" and "ties share the prize"; the partner page that says 20% where the code pays 50%.
5. Suites before: 1,927 pass, 5 fail (4 stale web tests, 1 random test). After: 2,010 pass, 0 fail (section 3).

## 2. How the work was done
- Baseline: all suites run per folder with `bun test --isolate`. The 5 failures were test problems, not game bugs (section 3).
- Model: `tools/qa-model.mjs` lists every combination of speed (6, 9, 12, 15 s), cards (1, 2), marking (auto, tap), join type (name, phone), rounds (1 to 5), size (free demo, every pack, too big), prizes and winners per prize (all 10 houses, 1 up to the most allowed, one too many), ties (2 to 10 guests on one call), who can join (anyone, list, employees only, mobile, ID, either, both), guest-list shapes (none, one, ten, eleven, repeated number, short number, no name, markup in a name), roles (Organizer, 7 Host attempts, Co-host, AI key, stranger across 8 actions), pause (every start inside a gap), the whole 10-person game, and, added after the audits, sequences (a bought pack waiting, a published game lowered to 10 people, erase before the start, eight Host adds at once, reuse, the winner override, the employees-only form). The expected side is written from `docs/boss/RULES_bingo.md`, `OPTIONS_TABLES.md`, `ALL_GAMES_CARRY_TAMBOLA_RULES.md` and `docs/BINGO_GAME.md` (the price table and the house table are read from the document text, not copied). The real Worker, prize engine, call schedule, price table and role checks are imported and run, nothing is re-implemented. Every difference gets a category: FAIL, GAP, NOT_LOGICAL, INCOMPLETE, GIBBERISH or NO_RESULT, with the file and line of the cause.
- Fixes: one commit per fix (two fixes share a commit where they are one change), in the repo's own style. For each, the new test was run first against the old code (it fails), then after the change (it passes). Some tests were also run with the old source file put back to show they fail again.
- Audits: three independent adversarial reviews by separate agents (security, correctness, wording). Their findings are in section 8; each fixed finding has its own commit and test.
- Real browser: Chromium, through the repo's own `tools/sim2` scenarios (section 7).
- Nothing was deployed, merged, paid or sent; the version file and the release-note numbering were not touched; no new dependency was added. The branch was cut from `boss/docs-2026-10-29` and only `qa/bingo-cloud-2026-10-29` is pushed.
