## Built (plain words)

1. **Wording in the console voice.** Every organizer, Host and Co-host screen now says "you" and "your". The count of first-person strings is **zero** in the console, the sign-in and pay pages, the organizer's pages and the mails the server sends (Tambola still keeps a list of 89). A test keeps it at zero. Left as they are on purpose: the agreement boxes (legal text), the partner pages, the AI assistant's own sentences. See `docs/CONSOLE_VOICE.md`.
2. **Start wording.** Step 1 says "The game starts by itself / The game begins at the time you set. You do nothing." Step 2 says "Numbers come by themselves / A new random number appears every 12 seconds." (the number follows the seconds the organizer picks; new games start at 12 seconds, was 9). **Only one of the four formats exists in this game** (see Needs, item 1).
3. **Invite people with just an ID.** One ID box. Every character that is not a letter or a number is dropped, then capitals, at upload, join, approval and matching: `A-1101`, `A 1101`, `A/1101` are one person. The owner's five lines are on the console screens word for word. Paste a list, upload a PDF or a CSV (only the ID column is used; names, e-mails and numbers are dropped in the browser). The join page has one box: "Enter your ID". A listed ID joins with no approval; an unlisted ID waits and the organizer or Host approves or declines. Family invite: a listed person can invite 1 to 10 (default 4). Only the organizer and Hosts see the full list and the invite log (who asked, through whom, who said yes or no; IDs only). "Anyone with the link" is gone from the console. Older games still match: an ID is also looked up in its old form (spaces dropped, `. _ - /` kept) for games created before `EMP_LEGACY_BEFORE_MS` (set to 1 Jan 2027 on purpose: safe if the release date slips); nothing stored is rewritten.
4. **No sign-in, payment or chat of its own.** There was no chat. Sign-in and payment: flags **`LOGIN_VIA_CONSOLE`** and **`PAY_VIA_CONSOLE`**, both **off**. When on: the old pay pages go to the console and the old pay routes answer a plain refusal (Razorpay's own notice to this game is never turned away); the sign-in page goes to home, the sign-in routes answer a plain refusal, and the game's console page shows "Please sign in at home" with two links. Nothing is deleted.
5. **Paid only through the console.** `POST /api/console/paid` takes the console's signed notice (Ed25519, 60 seconds, one use, this game's address, purpose "paid"), **once per order id**, forged, replayed, wrong-game and wrong-size notices refused; the game decides from its own tables (the payer owns the game, not cancelled, size matches, not yet paid). A game starts only when paid (an unpaid published game stays locked at its start time, as before). Dark without `CONSOLE_PUBKEYS`. See `docs/CONSOLE_PAY.md`.
6. **Deleted within 24 hours.** List, waiting requests, seats, ID look-ups, the wrong-ID counters and the log go with the players' data 20 hours after the game ends (the promise is 24). The wrong-ID counters were **not** erased before; now they are. Tests: `id-invite.test.ts` (erase, and the scheduled sweep), `privacy.test.ts` (unchanged). No page promises a PDF by e-mail: the report e-mail carries a link and the PDF is made on the organizer's own device (it exists).
7. **Fairness sentence** "No one knows the next number: not the organizer, not the host, not even the software." is on the console and the home page, and `packages/beacon/test/fairness.test.ts` proves its parts in code (a call's number comes only from one future beacon round; no clock, no random generator, no stored order).
8. **Rupee sign** on every screen, mail and AI sentence (the PDF writer keeps its own "Rs" fallback for fonts without the sign). Forbidden claims are absent and a test keeps them out. Billing line is exactly "We keep billing data for audit and tax reasons." Privacy line is now "designed to follow US privacy laws, the EU GDPR and India's DPDP" (home page, Trust page, the AI's facts). AI manual: family invite is 1 to 10.

## Needs (owner, or another repo)

1. **Two of the four start formats are missing.** This game only has "The game starts by itself" and "Numbers come by themselves" (the numbers come from a public random beacon on a fixed schedule). "You start the game" (press Start) and "You call the numbers" (press Next) do not exist in the engine, and the console does not offer them (a choice that does nothing must not be shown). The reference is Tambola's `go` and `next` signed messages (`control.ts`, `phone-runtime.ts`, `game.ts`, the host buttons): a press names a future beacon round so the sentence "No one knows the next number" stays true. It is a port of about 700 lines plus the host screens plus new fairness tests. **Decision for the owner: port it as its own piece of work?**
2. **The rest of the bridge.** Only `/api/console/paid` is open. Session, games list, billing, invited cards, hand-off and roles (the console's routes) are not built into this game, so `LOGIN_VIA_CONSOLE` must **stay off** until they are (otherwise nobody could sign in to this game's own console page). The contract kit (`apps/edge/test/console-contract`) is copied but not run here: it needs the full adapter.
3. **No `docs/CONTRACT_V1.md` exists in this repo.** Built to `corporate-console` branch `claude/console-game-pay-step-91b60a`, `docs/PAY_STEP.md` and `packages/bridge`, commit `2db8168` (cloned read-only). If that branch changes, copy the bridge again with `node tools/vendor-bridge.mjs --game <this repo>` from the console repo.
4. **Owner go, by hand, at deploy:** set `CONSOLE_PUBKEYS` (the console's public keys); later `PAY_VIA_CONSOLE=1`, then `LOGIN_VIA_CONSOLE=1` (names in `config/SECRETS_NAMES.md`). Moneypenny must be deployed first.
5. **Invoices.** A console payment makes no invoice in this game; Moneypenny and IT Services keep the payment record. Owner: confirm that is the rule. (Old pay code and its invoices stay behind the flag.)
6. **The AI assistant route** (`ai-go.ts`, AI link manual) still offers "Anyone with the link can join" when it makes a game, and still asks for names and mobile numbers in places. The AI route is a separate owner decision.
7. **Consent boxes** (K4 for players "my name (and my mobile number...)", K6 for the list "these names and numbers") still use the old words. They are legal text with a version the server checks; the lawyer or owner should give the new words for an ID-only game.
8. **Default speed 9 -> 12 seconds** for new games so the owner's sentence is true by default. Confirm.
9. **`EMP_LEGACY_BEFORE_MS`** is 1 Jan 2027 (generous on purpose). It can be lowered to the exact deploy time at release.
10. **Console voice, left as is:** the agreement boxes in `terms.html`, the partner pages, the AI assistant's own sentences.
11. The pay page's line "An RBI-regulated payment gateway trusted by businesses across the world" was not changed (not on the forbidden list); owner may want it softer.
12. Not run here: a real phone, real Razorpay, real Cloudflare, real drand. `pages.test.ts` (the console pages) takes more than 4 minutes: run it alone.
