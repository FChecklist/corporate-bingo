# Legal and policy pages: DRAFT FOR LAWYER REVIEW

These pages are written by the product team in plain language. They are drafts. A US lawyer and a CA (India) must read them before the first paid launch.

Pages (all under `apps/web/public/`):
- `terms.html`: Terms of use for organizers and players (no fee to play, 18+, voluntary, prizes by the organizer, no prize money held by us).
- `dpa.html`: Data processing terms (controller and processor, deletion within 24 hours, backups 7 days, 365-day records).
- `subprocessors.html`: Cloudflare, Resend, Razorpay, Google sign-in, drand, YouTube.
- `partner-terms.html`: Refer and earn terms (independent partner, no spam, tax, no commission without a buyer payment).
- `organizer-guide.html`: Rules for organizers (never ask players for a fee, check your state, tax on prizes).
- `legal.html`, `refund-policy.html`, `pricing.html`, `contact.html`, `about.html`: public information.

Open questions for the lawyer (see `docs/history/BINGO_SPEC_origin.md`, sections 13 and 15):
1. Is a closed-group Bingo with organizer-provided prizes outside US gambling and lottery rules in each state? The product never takes money from players and never holds or pays prize money.
2. US state privacy laws (California and others) for employees as players.
3. Selling software from India to US customers: tax position and payout rules for US partners.
4. Razorpay: international cards must be approved on the account before US dollar sales.

These pages must not be published as final until the lawyer has signed off.
